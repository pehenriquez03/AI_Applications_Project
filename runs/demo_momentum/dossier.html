Narrative template

What the paper proposes

The paper (Profitability of Momentum Strategies: An Evaluation of Alternative Explanations) proposes Each stock's past six-month return over the ranking period (month -5 to month 0) as the signal, sorted by deciles, favoring the P1 (past winners): decile with the highest past six-month returns. Rationale: Behavioral explanation: momentum profits arise from investors' biased interpretation of information (underreaction / delayed overreaction that is eventually reversed); the paper's evidence supports the delayed-overreaction behavioral models and rejects the Conrad and Kaul cross-sectional expected-return explanation.

What we implemented and changed

We adapted 5 fields and assumed 2. Adapted: breakpoints (no exchange or size breakpoints in our universe; equal-count sorts of eligible names); disfavored_group (policy: long-only, no short leg); universe_and_filters (policy: our 415-name S&P 500 survivor universe); price_floor (paper excludes stocks priced below $5; NOT applied: the snapshot's prices are split-adjusted, not as-traded, so a dollar floor would drop the wrong names (e.g. NVDA 2017-18)); sample_period (policy: our snapshot sample). Assumed: min_history_days set to 252; min_formation_days set to 15. Portfolios were formed in 132 months with 36.8 names on average.

What the backtest found

Over the sample the strategy returned 14.54% a year net of costs, against 12.71% for the equal-weight universe and 13.45% for SPY. Its Sharpe ratio was 0.68 against 0.65 for the equal-weight universe, and its maximum drawdown was -34.2%. The alpha t-statistic was 0.32; it is reported, not used as a gate. Robustness across costs, sub-periods and ranked groups gave a rho of -0.28.

What the simulation and economics found

Across the simulated paths, incremental fee income versus the equal-weight universe fund had a median present value of $2.0M, with a 5th to 95th percentile range of -$2.7M to $7.8M. The share of paths with positive NPV after research cost was 75.7%, and 47.7% once the historical excess return is removed. Break-even AUM is $900k; it is reported, not headlined.

How reliable the interpretation was

Across 2 extraction runs the material fields agreed on average 94.4% of the time, and evidence was valid for 100.0% of material fields with 0 check-pass disagreements.
